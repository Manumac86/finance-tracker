## 1. Getting Started

### Step 1: Creating Your Account

1. **Open FinTrack**
   - Navigate to https://fintrack.app in your web browser
   - Or open the app if you've installed it on your mobile device

2. **Click "Sign Up"**
   - Located in the top right corner of the landing page
   - Mobile: Tap the menu icon, then "Sign Up"

3. **Enter Your Email**
   - Type your email address
   - Ensure it's typed correctly - you'll need to verify it

4. **Create a Password**
   - Must be at least 8 characters
   - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
   - The strength indicator will show if your password is strong enough

5. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check your inbox for a verification email
   - Click the verification link
   - If you don't see it, check spam/junk folder

6. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Enter your name
   - Select your preferred currency (USD or EUR)
   - Choose your language (English or Spanish)

### Step 2: First-Time Onboarding

After account creation, you'll go through an interactive onboarding:

1. **Welcome Screen**
   - Read the welcome message
   - Click "Get Started" to begin

2. **Learn the Basics**
   - **What is a Budget?**
     - Read: "A budget helps you plan your spending and track where your money goes each month"
     - This helps you understand the core concept
   - Click "Next" to continue

3. **Set Your First Goal**
   - You'll see three goal types:
     - **Savings Goal**: For building wealth
     - **Debt Payoff**: For eliminating debt
     - **Spending Limit**: For controlling expenses
   - Select one that matches your priority
   - Enter:
     - Goal name (e.g., "Emergency Fund")
     - Target amount (e.g., $1,000)
     - Target date (optional)
   - Click "Save Goal"

4. **Try Adding a Transaction**
   - This is practice to familiarize you with the process
   - Enter:
     - Transaction name: "Sample Coffee Purchase"
     - Type: Select "Expense"
     - Amount: $5.00
     - Category: Select "Food & Dining"
   - Click "Save"
   - See how it appears in your transaction list

5. **Complete Setup**
   - Review what you've learned
   - Click "Complete Setup" to enter the main app

### Step 3: Navigating the Interface

After onboarding, familiarize yourself with the layout:

1. **Sidebar Navigation** (Desktop) / **Bottom Navigation** (Mobile)
   - **Dashboard**: Your financial overview
   - **Transactions**: All income and expenses
   - **Budgets**: Spending limits and tracking
   - **Goals**: Financial targets and progress

2. **Top Bar**
   - **Search**: Find any transaction quickly
   - **Profile Icon**: Access settings, theme, language

3. **Quick Actions** (Floating button on mobile, cards on desktop)
   - **Add Transaction**: Quick entry
   - **Create Goal**: New financial target
   - **Set Budget**: Spending limits

## 2. Understanding Your Dashboard

### Reading the Key Metrics Cards

Your dashboard displays four primary metrics at the top:

#### 1. Current Balance Card (Green)
- **Main Number**: Your net worth (Income - Expenses)
- **Percentage**: Savings rate
- **Color Coding**:
  - Green: Positive balance
  - Red: Negative balance
- **Example**: "$1,250" with "+12%" means you have $1,250 and saved 12% of income

#### 2. Income Card (Green with up arrow)
- **Main Number**: Total income for selected period
- **Period Label**: Shows if it's monthly/weekly/yearly total
- **Trend Arrow**: Shows if income increased or decreased

#### 3. Expenses Card (Red with down arrow)
- **Main Number**: Total spending for selected period
- **Period Label**: Current time frame
- **Important**: Negative number indicates money spent

#### 4. Goals Progress Card (Blue with target icon)
- **Fraction**: "2/5" means 2 goals completed out of 5 total
- **Label**: "goals completed"
- **Click to**: See all goals in detail

### Understanding the Financial Health Score

Located below the metrics cards:

1. **Score Display**: Large number (0-100) with color coding
   - 80-100: Excellent (Green) - "Great job! Keep up the excellent financial habits"
   - 60-79: Good (Blue) - "Try to increase your savings rate to 20%+"
   - 40-59: Needs Attention (Yellow) - "You're overspending! Review your budget"
   - 0-39: Critical (Red) - "Immediate action needed: Create a strict budget"

2. **Score Components** (4 metrics, each 25% of total):
   - **Savings Rate**: How much you save vs earn
   - **Budget Usage**: How well you stick to budgets
   - **Goal Progress**: Achievement of financial targets
   - **Emergency Fund**: Months of expenses saved

3. **Recommendations**: Personalized tips based on your score

### Reading the Balance Chart

The interactive chart shows your financial trend:

1. **Time Period Tabs**:
   - **Last Year**: Shows 12 monthly data points
   - **This Month**: Daily balance for current month
   - **Last Week**: 7-day balance history

2. **Chart Elements**:
   - **X-axis**: Time periods (months/days)
   - **Y-axis**: Balance amount in your currency
   - **Green Area**: Your balance over time
   - **Hover**: See exact balance for any point

3. **Understanding Trends**:
   - **Upward slope**: Growing wealth
   - **Downward slope**: Spending more than earning
   - **Flat line**: Stable balance

### Quick Actions Section

Four action buttons for common tasks:

1. **Add Transaction**: Opens transaction form
2. **Create Goal**: Start a new financial target
3. **Set Budget**: Create spending limits
4. **View Reports**: See detailed analytics

### Recent Activity

Shows your last 5 transactions:
- **Transaction Name**: What you bought/earned
- **Amount**: Green (+) for income, Red (-) for expense
- **Date**: When it occurred
- **Category Icon**: Visual category indicator

## 3. Adding Transactions - Step by Step

### Method 1: Quick Add (Recommended for Mobile)

1. **Tap the Floating "+" Button** (Mobile) or **Click "Add Transaction"** (Desktop)

2. **Fill in the Transaction Form**:

   **Step 1 - Basic Information:**
   - **Transaction Name** (Required)
     - What to enter: Descriptive name
     - Examples: "Grocery Store", "Monthly Salary", "Gas Station"
     - Tip: Be specific for better tracking

   - **Transaction Type** (Required)
     - Click one of two options:
       - **Income**: Money coming in (salary, gifts, refunds)
       - **Expense**: Money going out (purchases, bills, fees)
     - The form color changes: Green for income, Red for expense

   - **Amount** (Required)
     - Enter the number only (no currency symbol needed)
     - Use decimal point for cents: 15.99
     - The currency symbol appears automatically

   **Step 2 - Categorization:**
   - **Category** (Required)
     - Click the dropdown menu
     - Income categories:
       - Salary
       - Freelance
       - Business
       - Investments
       - Other Income
     - Expense categories:
       - Food & Dining
       - Transportation
       - Shopping
       - Entertainment
       - Bills & Utilities
       - Healthcare
       - Education
       - Housing
       - Other Expenses

   **Step 3 - Additional Details:**
   - **Date** (Optional - defaults to today)
     - Click the calendar icon
     - Select the actual transaction date
     - You can add past transactions

   - **Description** (Optional)
     - Add notes for context
     - Examples: "Weekly groceries", "Birthday gift for mom"
     - Helpful for remembering details later

3. **Save the Transaction**
   - Click the "Save" button
   - You'll see a success message
   - The transaction appears in your list immediately

### Method 2: Voice Input (Mobile Only)

1. **Open Add Transaction Form**
   - Tap the "+" button

2. **Tap the Microphone Icon**
   - Located next to the transaction name field
   - Your browser may ask for microphone permission - click "Allow"

3. **Speak Your Transaction**
   - Say clearly: "Coffee at Starbucks five dollars and fifty cents"
   - The app will parse:
     - Name: "Coffee at Starbucks"
     - Amount: $5.50
     - It will suggest category: "Food & Dining"

4. **Review and Confirm**
   - Check the parsed information
   - Make corrections if needed
   - Click "Save"

### Method 3: Bulk Import

Perfect for adding multiple transactions at once:

1. **Navigate to Transactions Page**
   - Click "Transactions" in the sidebar

2. **Click "Bulk Import" Button**
   - Located at the top of the page

3. **Choose Import Method**:

   **Option A: CSV Upload**
   - Click "CSV Upload" tab
   - Download the template CSV file
   - Fill in your transactions in Excel/Google Sheets
   - Format:
     ```
     Date,Description,Amount,Type,Category
     2025-06-01,Grocery Store,-45.50,expense,Food & Dining
     2025-06-02,Salary,3500.00,income,Salary
     ```
   - Click "Choose File" and select your CSV
   - Review the preview
   - Click "Import"

   **Option B: Quick Text Entry**
   - Click "Quick Text Entry" tab
   - Paste transactions in this format:
     ```
     Grocery Store -45.50
     Coffee Shop -5.00
     Salary +3500.00
     ```
   - Click "Parse Text"
   - Review and assign categories
   - Click "Create Transactions"

   **Option C: Manual Grid Entry**
   - Click "Manual Entry" tab
   - Click "Add Transaction" for each row
   - Fill in:
     - Name
     - Amount (positive for income, negative for expense)
     - Category (select from dropdown)
     - Date
   - Add as many rows as needed
   - Click "Create X Transactions"

### Editing a Transaction

1. **Find the Transaction**
   - Go to Transactions page
   - Use search or scroll to find it

2. **Click the Transaction Card**
   - The card expands to show details

3. **Click "Edit" Button**
   - Located in the card menu

4. **Modify Fields**
   - Change any information
   - All fields are editable

5. **Save Changes**
   - Click "Save Changes"
   - Confirmation message appears

### Deleting a Transaction

1. **Click the Transaction Card**
2. **Click "Delete" Button**
3. **Confirm Deletion**
   - Click "Yes, Delete" in the confirmation dialog
   - Transaction is removed from all calculations

## 4. Setting and Tracking Goals

### Creating Your First Goal

1. **Access Goal Creation**
   - Method 1: Click "Create Goal" in Quick Actions (Dashboard)
   - Method 2: Navigate to Goals page, click "Create Goal" button

2. **Select Goal Type**
   
   **Savings Goal** - Building wealth
   - For: Emergency funds, vacation savings, down payments
   - Example: Save $5,000 for emergency fund
   
   **Debt Payoff** - Eliminating debt
   - For: Credit cards, loans, mortgages
   - Example: Pay off $3,000 credit card debt
   
   **Spending Limit** - Controlling expenses
   - For: Reducing spending in specific categories
   - Example: Limit dining out to $200/month

3. **Fill in Goal Details**

   **For Savings Goals:**
   - **Goal Name**: "Emergency Fund"
   - **Target Amount**: $5,000
   - **Current Amount**: $500 (if you already have some saved)
   - **Target Date**: December 31, 2025 (optional)
   - **Description**: "Build 3-month emergency fund" (optional)

   **For Debt Payoff:**
   - **Goal Name**: "Credit Card Freedom"
   - **Current Debt Amount**: $3,000
   - **Target Date**: June 30, 2025
   - **Description**: "Eliminate high-interest debt"

   **For Spending Limits:**
   - **Goal Name**: "Reduce Dining Out"
   - **Category**: Food & Dining
   - **Monthly Limit**: $200
   - **Period**: Monthly

4. **Use Smart Suggestions**
   - Click "Suggestions" to see recommended amounts
   - Emergency Fund: Shows 3-month and 6-month expense calculations
   - Based on your actual spending data

5. **Save the Goal**
   - Click "Save Goal"
   - Goal appears in your goals list
   - Progress tracking begins immediately

### Understanding Goal Progress

Each goal card displays:

1. **Progress Bar**
   - Visual representation of completion
   - Green fill shows progress
   - Percentage displayed

2. **Current vs Target**
   - Shows "$1,500 of $5,000"
   - Updates automatically as you add transactions

3. **Time Remaining**
   - "45 days remaining" if target date set
   - Red text if overdue

4. **Status Indicators**
   - **In Progress**: Active goal
   - **Achieved**: Completed with celebration
   - **Overdue**: Past target date

### Tracking Goal Progress

1. **Automatic Updates**
   - Savings goals update when account balance changes
   - Debt goals update when you categorize debt payments
   - Spending limits track category expenses

2. **Manual Updates**
   - Click goal card
   - Click "Edit"
   - Update "Current Amount"
   - Save changes

3. **Goal Achievements**
   - Celebration animation plays
   - Confetti effect
   - Achievement recorded in history
   - Option to create next goal

## 5. Managing Budgets

### Creating a Budget (Step-by-Step)

1. **Start Budget Creation**
   - Dashboard: Click "Set Budget" in Quick Actions
   - Or: Navigate to Budgets page, click "Create Budget"

2. **Step 1: Basic Information**
   
   **Budget Name** (Required)
   - Be specific: "Monthly Groceries" not just "Food"
   - Examples:
     - "Monthly Groceries"
     - "Entertainment Budget"
     - "Gas and Transportation"
   
   **Description** (Optional)
   - Add context: "Including household items"
   - Helps remember what's included
   
   **Budget Type** (Select one):
   - **Category Budget**: 
     - For specific spending categories
     - Example: $500 for Food & Dining
   - **Total Budget**:
     - For overall spending limit
     - Example: $3,000 total monthly spending
   - **Custom Budget**:
     - For complex rules
     - Example: Combined categories

3. **Step 2: Period & Amount**
   
   **Budget Amount** (Required)
   - Enter your spending limit
   - Be realistic based on past spending
   
   **Period** (Select one):
   - **Weekly**: Resets every Monday
   - **Monthly**: Resets on the 1st
   - **Quarterly**: Every 3 months
   - **Yearly**: Annual budgets
   
   **Start Date**
   - Defaults to today
   - Can set future start date
   
   **End Date** (Optional)
   - Leave blank for ongoing budget
   - Set for temporary budgets

4. **Step 3: Alerts & Settings**
   
   **Enable Budget Alerts** (Toggle)
   - Turn on to receive notifications
   
   **Alert Threshold**
   - Default: 80%
   - Get warned before overspending
   - Can set 50%, 70%, 90%
   
   **Alert When Over Budget** (Toggle)
   - Immediate notification when exceeded
   
   **Rollover Settings**:
   - **No Rollover**: Fresh start each period
   - **Surplus Only**: Unused budget carries forward
   - **Deficit Only**: Overspending reduces next period
   - **Both**: Full rollover of surplus and deficit

5. **Create the Budget**
   - Review all settings
   - Click "Create Budget"
   - Budget monitoring begins

### Understanding Budget Cards

Each budget displays:

1. **Progress Bar**
   - Green: Under 50% spent
   - Yellow: 50-80% spent
   - Red: Over 80% or exceeded

2. **Financial Summary**
   - **Spent**: Current period spending
   - **Budget**: Total limit
   - **Remaining**: Available to spend

3. **Status Indicators**
   - **On Track**: Within safe spending range
   - **Warning**: Approaching limit
   - **Over Budget**: Exceeded limit

4. **Period Information**
   - Shows current period (e.g., "June 2025")
   - Days remaining in period

### Budget Alerts

You'll receive alerts when:

1. **Threshold Reached**
   - "You've used 80% of your Groceries budget"
   - Appears in notification panel
   - Email sent (if enabled)

2. **Budget Exceeded**
   - "You've exceeded your Entertainment budget by $50"
   - Red alert in dashboard
   - Immediate notification

3. **Period Ending**
   - "3 days left in your monthly budget period"
   - Reminder to review spending

### Managing Existing Budgets

1. **View All Budgets**
   - Go to Budgets page
   - See all active budgets
   - Filter by status or period

2. **Edit a Budget**
   - Click budget card
   - Click "Edit"
   - Modify any setting
   - Save changes

3. **Delete a Budget**
   - Click budget card
   - Click "Delete"
   - Confirm deletion
   - Historical data preserved

## 7. Quick Reference Guide

### Daily Tasks

**Morning Routine (2 minutes)**
1. Check Financial Health Score
2. Review any budget alerts
3. Note upcoming bills

**Adding Expenses (30 seconds each)**
1. Tap "+" button
2. Enter amount and name
3. Select category
4. Save

**End of Day (3 minutes)**
1. Add any missed transactions
2. Check goal progress
3. Review spending vs budget

### Weekly Tasks

**Sunday Planning (10 minutes)**
1. Review week's spending
2. Check all budget statuses
3. Adjust next week's spending
4. Update goal progress

**Transaction Review (5 minutes)**
1. Verify all transactions categorized
2. Look for unusual spending
3. Check for duplicate entries

### Monthly Tasks

**Month-End Review (15 minutes)**
1. Review Financial Health Score
2. Analyze spending by category
3. Celebrate goal achievements
4. Set next month's budgets
5. Adjust goals if needed

**Budget Planning (10 minutes)**
1. Review previous month's actuals
2. Identify overspending areas
3. Create/adjust budgets
4. Set spending priorities

### Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop)

- **Ctrl/Cmd + N**: New transaction
- **Ctrl/Cmd + G**: Go to goals
- **Ctrl/Cmd + B**: View budgets
- **Ctrl/Cmd + /**: Search
- **Esc**: Close modal/dialog

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Transaction Not Saving**
- Check all required fields filled
- Verify internet connection
- Refresh page and retry

**Wrong Balance Showing**
- Check transaction types (income/expense)
- Verify all transactions included
- Clear filters if active

**Budget Alert Not Working**
- Confirm alerts enabled
- Check threshold percentage
- Verify email not in spam

**Goal Not Updating**
- Ensure transactions categorized
- Manual update if needed
- Check date ranges

### Support Resources

**Get Help**
- In-app help: Click "Help" button
- Email: support@fintrack.app
- Response time: Within 24 hours

**Report Issues**
- Include screenshot
- Describe steps taken
- Note browser/device
- Mention error messages
