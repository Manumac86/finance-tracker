## Managing Your Debts

### Adding a New Debt

1. **Click** the "Add Debt" button
2. **Fill out** the debt information:

#### Basic Information
- **Debt Name**: Give your debt a recognizable name (e.g., "Chase Credit Card", "Car Loan")
- **Debt Type**: Select from:
  - 💳 **Credit Card**: Revolving credit accounts
  - 💰 **Personal Loan**: Fixed-term loans
  - 🏠 **Mortgage**: Home loans
  - 🎓 **Student Loan**: Education financing
  - 📄 **Other**: Any other type of debt
- **Lender**: The company or institution you owe money to

#### Financial Details
- **Original Amount**: The total amount you initially borrowed
- **Current Balance**: How much you currently owe
- **Interest Rate**: Annual percentage rate (APR)
- **Minimum Payment**: Required monthly payment amount

#### Payment Information
- **Payment Day**: Day of the month when payment is due (1-31)
- **Next Due Date**: When your next payment is due
- **Linked Account**: Connect to a bank account for tracking (optional)

#### Additional Information
- **Account Number**: For your reference (securely encrypted)
- **Notes**: Any additional information about this debt
- **Active Status**: Whether you're actively paying this debt

3. **Save** your debt information

### Viewing Your Debts

#### Debt List View
- **Grid Layout**: See debts as cards with key information
- **List Layout**: Compact view showing all debts in a table
- **Sorting Options**:
  - By name (A-Z or Z-A)
  - By balance (highest to lowest)
  - By interest rate
  - By due date
  - By date added

#### Filtering Debts
- **By Type**: Show only specific debt types
- **By Status**: Active or inactive debts
- **Search**: Find debts by name or lender

#### Debt Summary Dashboard
At the top of your debt list, you'll see:
- **💰 Total Debt**: Sum of all current balances
- **📊 Monthly Payments**: Total minimum payments
- **📈 Average Interest**: Weighted average rate
- **📅 Payoff Timeline**: Estimated completion date
- **🎯 Debt-to-Income Ratio**: Financial health indicator

### Editing Debt Information

1. **Find** the debt you want to edit
2. **Click** the three-dot menu on the debt card
3. **Select** "Edit"
4. **Update** any information
5. **Save** changes

### Debt Status Management

#### Active Debts
- Debts you're currently paying
- Included in calculations and summaries
- Show payment due alerts

#### Inactive Debts
- Paid-off debts or dormant accounts
- Kept for historical records
- Excluded from active calculations

To change status:
1. **Edit** the debt
2. **Toggle** the "Active" switch
3. **Save** changes

## Recording Payments

## Budget Integration

### Creating Debt Payment Budgets

The system now integrates seamlessly with your budgeting to ensure you allocate enough funds for debt payments while optimizing your payoff strategy.

#### Accessing Budget Integration
1. **Navigate** to "Financial Overview" or click "Create Budget" from debt dashboard
2. **Choose** "Debt Payment Budget" option
3. **Select** your strategy and let the system calculate optimal amounts

#### Budget Strategy Types

##### Minimum Payment Strategy
- **Purpose**: Cover required payments to avoid late fees
- **Amount**: Sum of all minimum payments
- **Best for**: Tight budgets, getting started with debt management
- **Calculation**: Automatically totals all your minimum payments

##### Accelerated Payment Strategy
- **Purpose**: Pay off debts faster with extra payments
- **Amount**: 150% of minimum payments (50% extra)
- **Best for**: Moderate income with some budget flexibility
- **Benefits**: Significantly reduces interest and payoff time

##### Custom Strategy
- **Purpose**: Tailored approach based on your specific situation
- **Amount**: You set the amount with system recommendations
- **Best for**: Advanced users who want full control
- **Features**: Smart suggestions based on your debt portfolio

#### Smart Budget Recommendations

The system analyzes your debt portfolio and provides intelligent recommendations:

##### High-Interest Debt Focus
- **Triggers**: When you have debts above 15% APR
- **Recommendation**: Extra budget allocation to highest-rate debts
- **Impact**: Maximum interest savings over time

##### Debt Consolidation Opportunities
- **Triggers**: Multiple debts with varying rates
- **Recommendation**: Budget for consolidation loan payments
- **Benefits**: Simplified payments, potentially lower rates

##### Aggressive Payoff Strategy
- **Triggers**: Low debt-to-income ratio (under 30%)
- **Recommendation**: Double minimum payments
- **Timeline**: Much faster debt freedom

#### Budget Impact Analysis

When creating a debt budget, you'll see:
- **Required vs. Budgeted**: Comparison of minimum payments to your budget
- **Extra Payment Amount**: How much extra you're allocating
- **Payoff Acceleration**: Estimated months saved
- **Interest Savings**: Money saved through extra payments

#### Budget Alerts and Monitoring

##### Overspending Alerts
- **80% Threshold**: Warning when approaching budget limit
- **100% Threshold**: Alert when budget is exceeded
- **Smart Suggestions**: Recommendations to get back on track

##### Under-spending Opportunities
- **Budget Surplus**: When you spend less than budgeted
- **Extra Payment Suggestions**: Apply surplus to debt reduction
- **Goal Achievement**: Faster progress toward debt freedom

### 💰 Payment Strategies

#### The Debt Avalanche Method
1. **List** all debts by interest rate (highest first)
2. **Pay minimums** on all debts
3. **Put extra money** toward highest interest debt
4. **Move to next** highest rate when first is paid off
5. **Repeat** until debt-free

**Best for**: Saving the most money on interest

#### The Debt Snowball Method
1. **List** all debts by balance (smallest first)
2. **Pay minimums** on all debts
3. **Put extra money** toward smallest balance
4. **Move to next** smallest balance when first is paid off
5. **Repeat** until debt-free

**Best for**: Building momentum and motivation

#### The Hybrid Approach
- **Combine** both methods strategically
- **Start with small wins** (snowball) for motivation
- **Switch to avalanche** for larger debts
- **Customize** based on your preferences

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is my debt information secure?**
A: Yes, all financial data is encrypted using bank-level security. Account numbers are specially encrypted and never stored in plain text.

**Q: Can I track joint debts with my spouse?**
A: Yes, use the family features to share debt tracking with family members while maintaining individual access controls.

**Q: What if I pay off a debt completely?**
A: The system automatically marks fully paid debts as inactive. They remain in your history for reference but are excluded from active calculations.

**Q: Can I import data from other apps?**
A: Yes, the system supports importing from CSV files and many popular financial apps. Check the import section for specific formats.

### Payment Questions

**Q: What if I miss a payment?**
A: Update your next due date in the debt details. The system will adjust calculations and show any overdue status.

**Q: Can I record partial payments?**
A: Yes, record any payment amount. The system tracks all payments regardless of size and updates your balance accordingly.

**Q: What about automatic payments?**
A: Connect your bank account to automatically import payment transactions, or set up reminders to manually record regular payments.

**Q: How do I handle interest rate changes?**
A: Edit your debt information to update the interest rate. This will affect future payoff calculations but won't change historical data.

### Calculation Questions

**Q: Why don't the calculators match my lender's website?**
A: Different calculators may use slightly different assumptions. Our calculations use standard formulas, but your lender may have specific terms or fees.

**Q: What if my minimum payment changes?**
A: Update the minimum payment amount in your debt details. This is common with credit cards as balances change.

**Q: How accurate are the payoff projections?**
A: Projections assume consistent payments and stable interest rates. Actual results may vary based on payment timing, rate changes, and fees.

**Q: Can I model multiple scenarios at once?**
A: Yes, use the payment calculator to explore different strategies. Compare scenarios to find the best approach for your situation.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Does this work on mobile devices?**
A: Yes, the debt management system is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and computers.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: All modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For best experience, keep your browser updated.

**Q: Can I export my debt data?**
A: Yes, export features are available for payment history, debt summaries, and progress reports in CSV and PDF formats.

**Q: What if I lose internet connection?**
A: Some features work offline, but payment recording requires an internet connection. Data is automatically synced when connection is restored.
